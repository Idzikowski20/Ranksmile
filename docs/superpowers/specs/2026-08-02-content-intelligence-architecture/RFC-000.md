# RFC-000 — What is Content Intelligence?

**Audience:** every engineer joining Ranksmile in 6–24 months  
**Length:** one page  
**Status:** companion to **RFC v1.0 FROZEN** — see [FREEZE.md](./FREEZE.md)  
**Not:** a feature list · not Coverage Engine marketing

---

## What is Content Intelligence?

The ability to turn an article into a **structured, queryable, explainable model of meaning** — then optimize, judge, and measure that model — instead of repeatedly re-reading HTML with ad-hoc prompts.

## What is the Content Compiler?

A pipeline (like a language compiler) that transforms editor content into an immutable **Canonical Content Model (CCM)** snapshot:

`Text → AST → IR → Passes → Constraints → CCM → Projections / Plans / History`

## Why CCM?

One Source of Truth for facts, entities, intents, evidence, structure, and reasoning.  
SEO score, Coverage %, AI Visibility, Benchmark gaps, and AO plans are **views** of that truth — not competing parsers.

## Why not HTML?

HTML is a presentation encoding. Semantic consumers that each parse HTML diverge, drift, and cannot share explainable diffs. HTML remains for editor, render, export, and allowlisted presentation gates only.

## Why Projections?

Coverage and Visibility are **named projections** over CCM. New needs (Trust, EEAT, …) add projections — they do not fork new “engines” that re-analyze the article.

## Why immutable?

Every compile produces a **snapshot** (`version` + `deterministicHash`). Judge, History, and Replay compare snapshots. There is no mutable “current model” that modules silently edit.

## Why Action Graph?

Optimization is an ordered, dependency-aware graph of structured edits (Recommendation DSL), version-locked to CCM — not a bag of missing keywords.

## Why DSL?

Planner, Judge, AO, and Intelligence lanes speak one closed op language (`ADD_FACT`, …) with expected score/visibility impact — not free-text recommendations that mean different things in each module.

## Why Pass Manager?

Domain and vertical logic (medical, legal, citations, …) plugs in as **passes/plugins** with dependencies — without rewriting the compiler core every quarter.

## What problems this solves

- Duplicate LLM “read the article” across AO / Judge / AIV / WIE  
- Unexplainable scores  
- Optimization without dependency order  
- Migration chaos (many SoTs)  
- Shortcut architecture (HTML parsers leaking into consumers)

## What it intentionally does NOT solve (v1)

- Perfect world knowledge / Wikidata completeness  
- Multi-site corpus / topical authority network (deferred — see backlog)  
- Replacing the TipTap editor  
- Cloning Surfer’s stack  
- Fully automatic publish without human/policy gates  

## Manifest

> **Coverage is a side effect of a good compile — not the product.**
